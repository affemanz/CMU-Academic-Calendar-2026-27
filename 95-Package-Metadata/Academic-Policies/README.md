# CMU Academic Calendar 2026/27: Academic Policies Package

This package contains **13 policy files** and **151 structured subsection records**. General policies are separated by major policy area. Degree-specific sections are separated into Bachelor of Education, Bachelor of Music Therapy, and Bachelor of Social Work files.

## Authority status

All calendar content is marked `official_transcription` and `validation_status: pending`. These files preserve the extracted source wording, but they have not yet been compared with the governing PDF or DOCX.

## Recommended AI use

1. Retrieve the narrowest relevant policy area.
2. Cite the calendar year, file-level section ID, and subsection title.
3. Keep definitions, exceptions, consequences, deadlines, and appeals with the policy they qualify.
4. Do not use policy rules to make a definitive individual eligibility or disciplinary decision.
5. If policies conflict or appear incomplete, cite the relevant passages and refer the question for institutional review.
6. Use `policy-sections.json` for search and filtering, but use the Markdown file as the citation context.
