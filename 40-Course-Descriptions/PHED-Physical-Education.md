## Physical Education

**PHED-1000 Foundations of Physical Education and Exercise Science** (3.0 credit hours) Introduction to physical education and exercise science as a profession and a discipline, including an overview of the sub disciplines, resources, and careers; a personal physical assessment; and principles for achieving physical fitness. The study of physical education will take into account the health of the whole person.

**PHED-2000 Growth and Motor Development** (3.0 credit hours) A study of physical growth and motor development from conception to adolescence, with implications for physical activity programs.

**PHED-2100 Coaching Theory and Practice** (3.0 credit hours) An introduction to the theoretical and practical aspects of coaching in the community and at the school level. It will include examination of topics of philosophical, psychological, ethical, and technical significance for coaching. The course will prepare students for certification in the National Coaching Certification Program at the Competition Introduction level.
