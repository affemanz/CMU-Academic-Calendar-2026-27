## 8. Bachelor of Education, Two-year After-Degree

##### Program Co-Chairs: Christine Kampen Robinson, PhD and Michelle Yaciuk, MMus

The Bachelor of Education is grounded in principles of peace-justice, foregrounds experiential learning inside and outside the classroom, and focuses on building key instructional skills with an understanding of systemic issues and personal agency. The all-grades curriculum focus forefronts creative pedagogical approaches to teaching different grade and skill levels and the concentrated practicum placements prepare graduates for teaching in classrooms.

*Admission Requirements:*

A recognized Bachelor’s degree conferred prior to beginning the program

A minimum adjusted grade point average of 2.5 (C+) based on the most recent 60 credit hours of university-level study

* *

*Graduation Requirements:*

60 credit hours fulfilling the program requirements as specified below

A minimum grade of C+ in all courses presented for graduation

** **

*Degree Regulations:*

1\. The Bachelor of Education is an accelerated 16-month after-degree program in which students will move through the program as a cohort. Students in the BEd after-degree program who complete the required courses as scheduled are considered full-time.

2\. A minimum grade of C+ is required in all Education courses (EDUC prefix). Teacher candidates will normally be permitted to repeat a given course only once and may not repeat more than 6 credit hours of education courses in the entire program. See the Bachelor of Education Academic Standing Policy section of this calendar for further details.

3\. Bachelor of Education teacher candidates are expected to demonstrate attitudes, values, and conduct that create safe spaces for peers and students. At CMU and in the school setting, teacher candidates are expected to maintain professional behaviour consistent with The Manitoba Teacher’s Society (MTS) Code of Professional Practice during classroom participation, in written assignments, presentations, and K-12 school contexts. See the Education Professional Readiness and Suitability policy section of this calendar for further details.

4\. The maximum time for completion of the Bachelor of Education is four years from date of the start of courses within the Bachelor of Education program. Any approved leaves of absence are counted within the four years.
