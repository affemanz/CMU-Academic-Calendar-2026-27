## 27. **Certificate **

##### Program Advisor: Coordinator of Academic Advising

#####

**27.1. **Certificate in General Studies, One-year

*Requirements*:

A minimum of 24 credit hours, including 3 credit hours in Biblical and Theological Studies.

*Regulations:*

1\. All courses toward the certificate must be taken in residence. Transfer credits cannot contribute to a certificate program.

2\. A student may earn a certificate over a one-year period of full-time study, or over several years of part-time study.

3\. The student must achieve a minimum GPA of 2.0.

4\. The student normally makes application for the certificate at the beginning of the semester after which the student hopes to receive it.
