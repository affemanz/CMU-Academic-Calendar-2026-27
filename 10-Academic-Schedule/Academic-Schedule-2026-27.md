# Academic Schedule 2026/27

Some programs (BEd and MBA) have alternate term dates. Students in these programs should consult their syllabi and the Student Hub. Intensive courses have unique dates.

#### Fall 2026

September 8 Opening convocation and academic orientation for all students

September 9 First day of fall semester

September 18 Last day of course change period for classes beginning in September

September 30 National Day for Truth and Reconciliation (university closed)

October 12 Thanksgiving Day (university closed)

November 9-13 Fall Reading Week (no classes)

November 11 Remembrance Day (university closed)

November 17 Last day for voluntary withdrawal from fall courses without academic penalty

December 9 Last day of regularly scheduled fall courses

December 10 (Thursday) Classes held according to a Monday schedule

December 11 Reading day (no classes)

December 12-23 Examination days (excluding December 13 and 20)

December 23 End of fall semester

December 24-January 3 University closed

#### **Winter 2027**

January 4 University services open

January 11 First day of classes for winter semester

January 14 Last day of course change period for classes beginning in January

February 15 Louis Riel Day (university closed)

February 15-19 Winter Reading Week (no classes)

March 12 Last day for voluntarily withdrawal from all full-year and winter courses without academic penalty

March 26 Good Friday (university closed)

April 9 Last day of regularly scheduled winter courses

April 12 (Monday) Classes held according to a Friday schedule

April 13-14 Reading days (no classes)

April 15-28 Examination days (excluding April 18 and 25)

April 28 End of winter semester

May 1 Convocation

#### **Spring 2027**

May 3 Spring semester begins (with various course start and end dates)

May 24 Victoria Day (university closed)

June 30 Spring semester ends

#### **Summer 2027**

July 1 Canada Day (university closed)

July 2 Summer semester begins (with various course start and end dates)

July 31 Summer semester ends

August 2 Terry Fox Day (university closed)
