## 5. Employment on Campus

CMU student employment positions are part-time, term positions (usually September to April) that offer financial support and flexible hours designed to fit with a student’s academic schedule. These roles provide valuable, real-world experience, opportunities for active learning, and meaningful ways to engage with the CMU community. CMU student employment provides jobs and income to between 70-100 students each year. A student working an average of 5 hours per week will earn approximately \$2,500 over the academic year.

Job applications submitted prior to the final day of the winter semester will receive priority consideration for returning students. In some cases, financial need could be considered when determining employment. The list of positions and the application is available on the CMU website (cmu.ca/student-life/employment).

#### *Eligibility Criteria:*

· Be a full-time student.

· Submit an application for consideration, including a resume.
